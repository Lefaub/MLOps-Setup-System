**How to read the scales.** For Q7 and Q8, 1 means very clear or very relevant. For Q13, 1 means never and 5 means very frequently. For Q14–Q16, 1 means very much and 5 means not at all. For Q17, 1 means highly scalable; for Q18, 1 means very easy and 5 means very difficult. The complete wording and both endpoint labels are in the questionnaire. Quartiles are category values, not interpolated scores.

**Context versus outcomes.** On relevance, 7 of 11 chose 4 or 5 toward “not relevant”; one participant skipped the item. On setup time, 8 of 12 chose 1 or 2 toward “very much”. These are separate marginals. Only Q14–Q18 enter the five-outcome analysis. The manuscript reverses Q14–Q17, so Table 6 is in the analysis direction; the counts above and in Table 5 are in the original direction. Q18 retains its original direction throughout.

**Interest in testing (Q9).** Five answered yes, one no, five maybe and one gave another response. Its wording is withheld to avoid identification. These are intentions, not observed adoption.
